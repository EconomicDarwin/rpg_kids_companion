# Player Journal (kid-facing)

The source for the app's Our Story tab. One entry per played session, authored by
Dad and Claude after each game. This is a rewrite for young readers, never a copy
of the GM session log.

Rules for entries:

- Heading format is exactly `## Session N — Title`. The title is the kid-facing
  title, not the GM log title.
- Short kid-sized sentences. Roughly 2 to 8 of them, depending on how much the
  session earned. A session with three big reveals is allowed to run long.
- No em-dashes and no semicolons in the entry text (the heading line is exempt).
- Only table-revealed facts. The export cross-checks that every session log in the
  canon repo has exactly one entry here, and refuses to run otherwise.
- An entry may carry one `Art:` line naming a picture under the canon
  `05_Asset_Pipeline/Final_Art/`. The export downscales it for the tablets.
  **Look at the picture before you name it here.** Canon art is drawn for the GM
  and can show things the girls have not learned yet. Odbert's box art is the
  standing example: the letter in it plainly reads "the glyph leads to the
  moon-stone," so it cannot ship until Moira's reveal is played.

## Session 1 — We Are the Lima Sisters

Art: locations/whispering_caverns_usurper_mural.png

We cleared the rats out of Odbert's basement, saved Roger, and followed the tunnels
down into a huge cave. A treasure chest there held two magic rings.
We put them on and they glowed. We are sisters of the Lima Clan! Deeper in we found
a letter from Mom. She is alive and she is leading a bad man far away to keep us
safe. A shiny stone lit up the wall and showed our family long ago, with a dark
shadow watching them. Its name is the Eclipse.

## Session 2 — Odbert's Secret Box

Art: equipment/odberts_box_contents.png

We looked around town and stopped at Odbert's shop. His old money box had our clan
sign painted on the lid. It had a secret bottom! Inside was an old book about
shadow monsters and a torn letter from Mom. Odbert said Mom left the box with him
a long time ago to keep it safe.

## Session 3 — The Fire in Rivenshore

Art: locations/moiras_sanctuary.png

Fires broke out all over town but we put them out. We saved Roger again, this time
from under a falling light. Poor Roger! We kept Bree safe at Moira's sanctuary.
Moira gave us our first spell cards. Rainbow got Crescent Moon Shield and Sky-Saver
got Meteor Strike!

## Session 4 — The Door Under the Town

Art: locations/vault_door_waking.png

We practiced our new spells and helped fix up the whole town. The sheriff paid us
in gold and shiny gems. Then Rainbow Fairy chose Rainbow and Starry the star-kitten
chose Sky-Saver. At tea Moira showed us a torn
paper about the Moon-Stone. Our prism made secret words appear on it. Pippa at the
Archives let us go down to the old vault. Sky-Saver put her sun-jewel in the door
and it opened one inch. Cold air came out and we could hear music far below!

## Session 5 — The Knights Who Waited

Art: scenes/hollow_watch_kneels.png

We went down the mossy stairs into old ruins under the town. We found a shiny
silver brooch tucked in a wall. Then we came to a huge hall with our family crest
on it. Three knight ladies were still guarding it after all this time. Two of them
saw our rings and knelt right down. The third one had dark magic on her, so we had
to fight. Our very last hit knocked the dark magic out of her and healed her! Her
name is Perriglossa and now she comes with us. We camped inside a ring of glowing
Star-Seed briars. A music box had played for us the whole way down, and when we
picked it up it finally stopped. It was waiting for its owners to come home! Then
we lifted the Moon-Stone together, four hands at once. A stone queen on the wall
opened her eyes!
